How to play

Three taps deep

  1. Look at the opposition. Their exact lineup is already standing on the right-hand sand before you spend anything.
  2. Spend your larvae. Drag specimens from the tray onto your half — or tap a card, then tap the sand.
  3. Send them in. The fight runs itself. You win it in the arranging, not the watching.
  4. Keep what you beat. Clearing a chamber hands you one of the species that was just fighting you. That is the only way to get them.

Stars come from survivors, not speed — three stars needs 60% of your colony still standing.
